## What this domain is responsible for

This domain is responsible for delivering structured knowledge, progression through teachings, and the logic around what content is available to whom and when.

## What it does not own

- access-code creation
    
- rank calculation
    
- social posting
    
- challenge participation
    
- event RSVP
    

## Main entities

- teaching
    
- lesson or content unit
    
- category
    
- path
    
- progress record
    
- unlock requirement
    

## Key relationships

This domain connects strongly to:

- Access and Gating
    
- Discipline and Progression
    
- Identity and Membership
    

## Modeling note

Teachings should be treated as a structured knowledge system, not as loose CMS pages only.
