# Community and Social

## Purpose

This domain defines how members interact with each other inside MONARQ OS.

## What this domain owns

- posts
    
- comments
    
- reactions
    
- community feed logic
    
- social interaction records
    
- visible member activity91.jpg+1
    

## What this domain is responsible for

This domain is responsible for member-to-member interaction, social expression, shared wins, reflections, and the sense of club belonging.

## What it does not own

- identity source of truth
    
- challenge scoring rules
    
- event scheduling
    
- teaching content structure
    
- product sales logic
    

## Main entities

- post
    
- comment
    
- reaction
    
- feed item
    
- community tag or category
    

## Key relationships

This domain connects strongly to:

- Identity and Membership
    
- Discipline and Progression
    
- Events and Participation
    
- Admin and Operations
    

## Modeling note

Community data should be modeled as social interaction records, not just as generic text blobs attached everywhere.
