

MONARQ OS should be built in controlled phases.

## Phase 1 — Foundation

Define doctrine, audience, promise, domains, V1 scope, visual direction, and system architecture.

## Phase 2 — System design

Map the app structure, member journeys, access model, admin logic, content model, and community model.

## Phase 3 — V1 build

Build the first real product:

- onboarding,
    
- home,
    
- habits,
    
- feed,
    
- teachings,
    
- progression,
    
- drop center,
    
- and admin basics.
    

## Phase 4 — Test and refine

Audit UX, retention, flow, bugs, permissions, moderation, and content clarity.

## Phase 5 — Launch

Private rollout, early member feedback, internal fixes, brand/content alignment, first real usage cycle.

## Phase 6 — Expansion

Add deeper social, AI, events, commerce sophistication, and richer member intelligence.

The roadmap should communicate direction, priorities, and milestones over time, which is why phases should stay outcome-based rather than turning into a giant task list.
