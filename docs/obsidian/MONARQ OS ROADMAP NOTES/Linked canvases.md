

The roadmap canvas is the top-level command center. The other canvases exist to go deeper into specific areas.

## ROADMAP

Purpose:

- overall direction,
    
- top-level planning,
    
- build order,
    
- and strategic overview.
    

## DOMAINS

Purpose:

- define the major parts of the MONARQ OS product,
    
- what belongs where,
    
- and where boundaries exist.
    

## FEATURES

Purpose:

- break domains into actual user-facing capabilities,
    
- flows,
    
- modules,
    
- and priorities.
    

## DESIGN

Purpose:

- define visual direction,
    
- app feel,
    
- brand atmosphere,
    
- component patterns,
    
- and screen-level thinking.
    

## SECURITY

Purpose:

- define access control,
    
- permissions,
    
- gated membership logic,
    
- moderation,
    
- trust,
    
- and platform protection.
    

The relationship should be:

- Roadmap decides direction.
    
- Domains decides structure.
    
- Features decides what exists.
    
- Design decides how it feels.
    
- Security decides how it is protected.
    

This keeps the MONARQ OS brain modular and prevents everything from collapsing into one overloaded planning board. That kind of modular roadmap structure aligns better with long-term product planning than a single flat brainstorming document.aha+1
