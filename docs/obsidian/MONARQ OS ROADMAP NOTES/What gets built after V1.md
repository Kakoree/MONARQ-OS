

After V1, MONARQ OS should expand depth, not just add random features.

Post-V1 buildout should include:

## Deeper social systems

- Direct messaging
    
- Group chats
    
- Circles / pods / accountability groups
    
- Better member profiles
    
- Richer social actions
    

## Smarter progression

- Advanced levels
    
- Milestone rewards
    
- Member ranks
    
- Unlockable pathways
    
- Status tied to behavior, not just purchases
    

## AI layer

- Personal AI coach “The Monk”
    
- Personalized nudges
    
- Reflection prompts
    
- Suggested teachings / habits
    
- Progress summaries
    

## Live experiences

- Events calendar
    
- RSVP system
    
- Calls / live rooms
    
- Local meetups or chapters
    

## Commerce expansion

- Smarter drop sequencing
    
- Tier-based access
    
- Loyalty / member perks
    
- Referral systems
    
- Better integration between club status and commerce
    

## Intelligence and retention

- Better analytics
    
- Churn detection
    
- Member lifecycle tracking
    
- Admin dashboards
    
- Experiment tracking for challenges, content, and engagement
    

After V1, every new layer should deepen identity, retention, and exclusivity — not just increase surface-level feature count.
