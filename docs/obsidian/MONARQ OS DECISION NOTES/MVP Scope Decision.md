# MVP Scope Decision

## Status

Accepted

## Context

MONARQ has a broad brand vision, but the team is small and building progressively. Trying to ship the full vision immediately would increase cost, risk, and implementation drift.

## Decision

The MVP will focus on the smallest version of MONARQ that proves the core model:

- gated access
    
- daily member experience
    
- community layer
    
- teachings
    
- progression
    
- events
    
- member identity
    
- basic drop integrationcreworklabs+1
    

## Why this was chosen

A focused MVP is more likely to ship, be testable, and create a strong base for future expansion. Overbuilt MVPs usually create delay and unnecessary complexity.miro+1

## Alternatives considered

- building the full club ecosystem at once
    
- prioritizing only commerce first
    
- adding advanced AI and deep automation immediately
    

## Consequences

## Pros

- Faster path to working product
    
- Lower cost
    
- Clearer validation
    
- Less rework from premature complexitymvp+1
    

## Cons

- Some vision elements wait until later
    
- The product may feel intentionally incomplete at first
    

## Revisit later if

- MVP retention is strong
    
- users clearly demand second-wave features
