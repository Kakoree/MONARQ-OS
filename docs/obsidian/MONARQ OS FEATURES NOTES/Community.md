# Community

## Purpose

This feature area handles the social layer of MONARQ OS.

## Core job

Let members share wins, reflections, updates, and support in a way that feels elevated and intentional rather than noisy or cheap.

## Must include

- Community feed
    
- Create post
    
- Commenting
    
- Reactions or lightweight engagement
    
- Ability to share wins / progress
    
- Basic filtering or grouping if needed
    
- Member identity visible on posts91.jpg+2
    

## Desired feeling

Community should feel like a private members club feed, not like public social media. The tone should be high-trust, aspirational, and constructive.51.jpg+1

## Non-goals for V1

- DMs
    
- Voice rooms
    
- Livestreaming
    
- Algorithmic feed ranking
    
- Endless engagement loopscreworklabs+1
    

## Success looks like

Members can post, respond, and feel connected to the club without the experience becoming cluttered or distracting.[kwiqwork](https://kwiqwork.com/blog/write-mvp-spec-developers-understand)

## Build note

Community should support belonging and momentum, but should not dominate the product more than discipline, growth, and identity.
