
# Challenges and Progression

## Purpose

This feature area drives commitment, growth, accountability, and status.

## Core job

Give members a way to join challenges, track progress, gain XP or levels, and feel that their effort leads to visible advancement.

## Must include

- Challenge list
    
- Challenge detail page
    
- Join challenge flow
    
- Progress tracking
    
- XP or points system
    
- Level / rank logic
    
- Unlock signals tied to progress
    
- Leaderboard link or integrated ranking71.jpg+1
    

## Desired feeling

This should feel motivating, prestigious, and structured. It should feel like earned progression, not childish gamification.31.jpg+1

## Non-goals for V1

- Overly complex game mechanics
    
- Seasonal battle pass logic
    
- Virtual economy
    
- Too many scoring systems
    
- Excessive animations or badgespeiko+1
    

## Success looks like

Members understand what they can join, what they are progressing toward, and why their consistency matters inside MONARQ.[makemyprd](https://www.makemyprd.com/templates/mvp-spec-template-for-lovable-project)
