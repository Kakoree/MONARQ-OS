# Commerce and Drops

## Purpose

This feature area handles the product and access-commerce layer of MONARQ.

## Core job

Support jewelry drops and key drops in a way that fits the club identity and gated-access model.

## Must include

- Drop listing
    
- Drop detail page
    
- Regular jewelry drops
    
- Key drop concept
    
- Redemption-linked access where needed
    
- Availability / status visibility
    
- Clear relationship between buying and membership access
    

## Desired feeling

Commerce should feel exclusive and integrated into the world of the club. It should not feel like a standard e-commerce store pasted into the app.101.jpg+1

## Non-goals for V1

- Full marketplace
    
- Complicated discount systems
    
- Broad catalog shopping experience
    
- Massive checkout customization
    
- Resale / exchange systemsmvp+1
    

## Success looks like

Members understand what is available, what drops matter, and how key drops connect to access and identity inside MONARQ.[kwiqwork](https://kwiqwork.com/blog/write-mvp-spec-developers-understand)

## Build note

Commerce should support the brand and club system, not overpower it. It is an important layer, but not the whole product.

## Recommendation
